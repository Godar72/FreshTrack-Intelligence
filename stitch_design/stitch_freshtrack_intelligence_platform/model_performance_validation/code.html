Academic Governance & Protocol Integrity

Methodology, Validation Rigor & Operational Boundaries

01

Data Leakage Prevention

Strict Chronological holdout validation: training through July 16, 2017; validation July 17 through August 15, 2017. Features, categorical target encodings, and temporal aggregations are constructed strictly using training data prior to each evaluation slice, ensuring zero lookahead leakage into the 30-day validation window.

verified_userZero lookahead temporal integrity
02

Model Selection Rationale

HistGradientBoosting achieved the lowest WAPE (10.69%) across the 270 perishable evaluation samples, demonstrating superior non-linear pattern capture over regularized linear models (Ridge 13.08%) and outperforming Random Forest (11.29%).

03

Known Limitations & Boundaries

Evaluation is strictly confined to Store 1 (Quito, Pichincha, Ecuador) holdout partition (270 samples). Results demonstrate relative model performance under controlled historical conditions and do not claim universal generalization across differing geographical store clusters.
